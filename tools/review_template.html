Stop Box Review

off

Each card is one kind of unit: the side view on the left with the ground line and a 1.8 m Helldiver, the top view on the right with the unit's root as a cross. Blue boxes sit inside the solid; green ones are plates on flat faces and boxes laid over thin sheets (they may stand a little outside). A line would stop on either. Click a card to leave that kind out; click again to take it back. A kind the height rule drops is greyed; clicking it keeps it anyway. Some kinds are open underneath as modelled (a walkway on stilts, an arch, a tangle of roots): their cards have a switch. Open underneath puts boxes only where the model is closed; Filled solid treats everything under it as rock, which is right when the game does (a root wall, a debris cover). By its outline fills whatever lies behind the thing seen from both sides and from above, hollow or not: a ruined building or a car as one block. New kinds that are mostly air as modelled start on it. When you are done, copy the list at the bottom and send it back.

Never candidates

Skipped before any boxes were made: too big (a backdrop), too small, a name that lets rounds through, or too thin for a box to fit.
