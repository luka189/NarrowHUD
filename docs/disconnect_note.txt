For the Nexus pinned post / replies to the disconnect reports (1.5.29)

If you get dropped from a host's mission while the others stay in, this is what would help most, in this order:

1. Update to 1.5.29 and play as before. Then post the two logs from %APPDATA%\Arrowhead\Helldivers2\ (narrowhud.log
   and chef_loader.log) with the clock time of the drop, the exact text of the error, and whether the rest of the
   squad stayed in. 1.5.29 logs more than 1.5.27 did: whether the mission froze for you before the drop and for how
   long, what the engine was doing, and the ping to the host if the game lets us read it.

2. If you drop every time: two research builds are in the Files tab, R0 and R1. Install one of them instead of
   the mod (one at a time), play one mission with each, and post the logs from both. R0 reads nothing and draws
   nothing (you'll see no HUD at all; the loader is there and the mod counts frames). R1 reads everything and draws
   nothing (no HUD either). If you still drop with R0, it's the loader being there at all; with R1 but not R0, it's
   the mod's reads of the game; only with the normal build, it's the drawing. Put the normal build back afterwards.

Nothing in this touches the game's network: the mod only reads and draws. These builds exist so the logs can tell us
which part the game minds.
