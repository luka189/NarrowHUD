NarrowHUD: Nexus Mods page text
================================

Name: NarrowHUD
Version: 2.0.0
Category: User Interface (or Gameplay > HUD, whichever the site lists)
Main file: NarrowHUD-v2.0.0.zip (archive the older main files and the R0 / R1 research builds)
Requirements (the "Requirements" box): Chef Shared Loader (nexusmods page of Th3chef's loader), "1.2.3 or newer"

Summary (the short line under the title, at most 350 characters):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A narrow ammo and heat readout by your crosshair: bar, count, magazines, modes, stims, grenades, backpack, health,
stamina, emplacement guns. Drop lines for launchers, throw lines for stratagems and every grenade, your map pin and
where you died. Presets or your own layout from the online editor; a rule per part for when it shows. Reads only.

Description (paste into the BBCode editor):
-------------------------------------------
[size=5][b]NarrowHUD[/b][/size]
[i]Narrow enough to not limit the Design Space.[/i]

A small ammo and heat readout right by your crosshair, so you never have to look at the corner of the screen
mid-fight.

[size=4][b]NEW in 2.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Four new layouts[/b] (the horizontal stack, the arcs, the vertical pair, the block) that hold together: parts pin to each other, so hiding or resizing one keeps the rest in order. A Spread slider opens or closes the pair around the crosshair. The editor has pins, snapping, a Share link and a Help page.
[*][b]A rule for every part:[/b] always, while not full, on a change, while aiming, or a mix.
[*][b]Motion:[/b] parts slide, bars ease, short flashes mark the things that matter. Off, quick or normal.
[*][b]Colours now look as they are meant to[/b], on every planet.
[*][b]Emplacement guns[/b] show their rounds while you're seated.
[*]A throw line for [b]every grenade[/b]; bar edges, brackets and shade; the sniper rifles' crosshair in third person.
[*]The settings menu regrouped. Your settings carry over, except the layout: pick yours in F9 (Custom users: Custom layout again; your file is untouched).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Make your own layout[/b][/size]
[b][url=https://luka189.github.io/NarrowHUD/editor/]>> Open the NarrowHUD layout editor <<[/url][/b]
Drag every part anywhere, pin parts to each other, make bars vertical or curved, and see how each weapon will look. Then:
[list=1]
[*]Download your layout file from the editor.
[*]In the game, F9 > NarrowHUD > [b]Layout: Custom layout[/b].
[*]Put the file in [b]%APPDATA%\Arrowhead\Helldivers2[/b] as [b]chef_data_luka189_2fnarrowhud__layout.lua[/b].
[*]Press the "Keys: reload layout file" key, or start a mission.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What it shows[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Ammo[/b]: a tick per round (a bar for big magazines), the count, your spare magazines. Orange when low, red when empty.
[*][b]Heat[/b] and [b]charge[/b] for laser, plasma and charge weapons; [b]lock-on[/b] for the Spear and WASP.
[*][b]Backpack weapons[/b]: what's loaded and what's in the pack.
[*][b]Fire modes, weapon modes, underbarrels[/b] and the rate of fire, as words and as the game's own icons.
[*][b]Stims, grenades[/b], your [b]backpack[/b], [b]health and stamina[/b], a stim timer, reload progress.
[*][b]Emplacement guns[/b] while you're seated.
[*][b]Drop lines[/b] for launchers, rockets and plasma; [b]throw lines[/b] for stratagem balls, grenades and C4.
[*]Your [b]map pin[/b] and [b]where you last died[/b], with their distance.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Layouts[/b][/size]
The horizontal stack, the arcs, the vertical pair, the block, or your own from the editor. Every part has its own rule for when it shows.

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size] (F9 > NarrowHUD)
Layout, size, offsets, Spread and opacity; a Show rule per part; the look (bar edges, shade, colours); extras (stim timer, warnings, reload line, idle dimming); motion; the world (crosshair, markers, lines); your keys as bound in the game.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Chef Shared Loader[/b] [b]1.2.3 or newer[/b], with the highest priority in your mod manager.
[*]Add the NarrowHUD zip in your mod manager and enable it.
[*]Play. Settings are in F9.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
NarrowHUD [b]only reads[/b] game state through Chef Shared Loader; it never writes to the game. It learns which weapon is in your hand the first time you fire it (most are known already). After a game patch it checks its data and stays off, telling you, if something it can't check has changed.

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]A weapon doesn't show.[/b] Fire it once.
[b]It shows the wrong weapon.[/b] Fire the one you're holding. If it keeps happening: F9 > "Testing: forget this weapon", press it holding the weapon, fire.
[b]I changed my fire button or swap keys in the game.[/b] Set the same under Keys in F9. If your mouse wheel doesn't swap weapons in the game, set "Keys: wheel swaps weapons" to No, or a scroll can switch the HUD to the wrong weapon.
[b]"NarrowHUD is off: the game was updated".[/b] Wait for an update, or turn on "Testing: any game version" at your own risk.
[b]Over the map?[/b] 2.0 draws in the game's UI layer so the colours are right. "Look: in the game's UI layer" off puts it back under.
[b]Multiplayer?[/b] Yes, hosting and joining. Only your own weapons and pack.
[b]Performance?[/b] About 0.2 ms a frame.

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]PlayStation pads are untested (Xbox-style pads work). 4K, render scale under 100 % and HDR are untested.
[*]The Arc Thrower and the Blitzer have no ammo to show; the Solo Silo's remote shows nothing.
[/list]
Bug reports: see the pinned post (both logs, please).

Changelog for the Nexus "Changelogs" tab (2.0.0)
------------------------------------------------
Your settings carry over but the layout choice. The F9 menu is regrouped, with short labels. Old Custom layout
files still load.
- Four new layouts (the horizontal stack, the arcs, the vertical pair, the block) that hold together: parts pin to
  each other, so hiding or resizing one keeps the rest in order. A Spread slider opens or closes the pair around
  the crosshair. The old Under / Beside / Corner / Curved pair are gone: pick your layout in F9 (Custom users:
  Custom layout again; the file is untouched).
- The layout editor: pins, snapping, a grid, a Share link for your layout, a Help page.
- A Show rule for every part: always, while not full, on a change, while aiming, or a mix.
- Motion: parts slide, bars ease, and short flashes mark a bar at zero, the last magazine, an overheat, a stim or
  grenade used, stamina empty or full. Off, Quick or Normal.
- Colours now look as they are meant to, on every planet. The opacity slider works across its whole range.
- Emplacement guns: the HMG, Anti-Tank and Grenadier emplacements show their rounds while you're seated.
- A throw line for every grenade kind.
- Bar edges (none, outline, or the game's brackets), the shade behind the bars, the weapon mode and fire mode as
  separate parts with their icons, the scope's zeroing as text or icon, a tick per use on the backpack bar.
- A crosshair for the sniper rifles aimed in third person.
- Fixed: the overheat flash looked purple; the vertical backpack bar emptied from the wrong end; a wrong grenade
  line for a moment after a throw.
- Needs Chef Shared Loader 1.2.3 or newer, as before.

Changelog for the Nexus "Changelogs" tab (1.5.38)
-------------------------------------------------
- THE DISCONNECTS: found by bisection today. When you play in someone else's game, NarrowHUD listed, sorted and
  read the host's objects (600-odd of them, once a second) to find any weapon of yours the host might own. With
  that on, the client lost its host 12 to 17 minutes into every mission; with it off, missions of 16 and 28 minutes
  went through clean. 1.5.38 never touches the host's objects. Nothing you see changes: your weapons, the ones from
  your hellpods too, belong to you as far as the game is concerned and are read as before.
- The interim 1.5.36's two switched-off reads (the ground-weapon fix, the underbarrel icon) are back: they were
  not it.
- If you still get dropped on 1.5.38: narrowhud.log and chef_loader.log (%APPDATA%\Arrowhead\Helldivers2) with
  the clock time, please. The logs record the minutes before a drop.
- Thank you to everyone who posted logs and ran the test builds today.
