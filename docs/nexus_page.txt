NarrowHUD: Nexus Mods page text
================================

Name: NarrowHUD
Version: 2.4.0
Category: User Interface (or Gameplay > HUD, whichever the site lists)
Main file: NarrowHUD-v2.4.0.zip (archive the older main files and the R0 / R1 research builds)
Requirements (the "Requirements" box): Chef Shared Loader (nexusmods page of Th3chef's loader), "1.2.3 or newer"

Summary (the short line under the title, at most 350 characters):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A narrow ammo and heat readout by your crosshair: bar, count, magazines, modes, stims, grenades, backpack, health,
stamina, emplacement guns. Drop and throw lines with a landing mark (red on a bug hole or fabricator), your map pin
and where you died. Presets or your own layout from the online editor; a rule per part for when it shows. Reads only.

Description (paste into the BBCode editor):
-------------------------------------------
[size=5][b]NarrowHUD[/b][/size]
[i]Narrow enough to not limit the Design Space.[/i]

A small ammo and heat readout right by your crosshair, so you never have to look at the corner of the screen
mid-fight.

[size=4][b]NEW in 2.4[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Shell flash:[/b] each round fired flicks out of the ammo bar in its colour, like a shell thrown out (F9 > Layout, with its length).
[*][b]Hit trail:[/b] what each hit took stays red a moment, then drains away, hit by hit; none on a red health bar (F9 > Visibility, how long it stays).
[*][b]Hatched shade:[/b] the game's own diagonal stripes behind a bar's empty part, straight or curved (F9 > Look > Shade behind bars).
[*][b]Flash once:[/b] the low-health warning as one flash, then simply red (F9 > Visibility > Warning pulses).
[*]The reload line runs until the gun is ready (a round chambered from empty; the Apex, the AMR); the SG-451 Cookout shows both tubes; firing with F9 open no longer hides your weapon.
[/list]

[size=4][b]NEW in 2.3[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]The weapon in your hand by its model:[/b] shown the moment you draw it, before any shot; two weapons on one hand pose (a pistol and a rifle on a controller) told apart without a key.
[*][b]The landing mark on the game's own aim point:[/b] the ground wherever you have looked is known, the line aims where the crosshair really points, and ground you haven't looked at follows the slope.
[*][b]Hide at a terminal[/b] (F9 > Visibility). The HUD hides at once, health and stamina with it in step.
[*][b]Side Up / Down[/b] for a layout's parts: the horizontal layout moves down as a whole while you aim.
[*]Your own Helldiver found by position (no more takeover by an ally behind you); lines keep their width; a kit's magazine size never shrinks; F9's on / off switch works at once on Chef 1.5.
[/list]

[size=4][b]NEW in 2.2[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Drop line start[/b] where the shot has fallen a set distance below a straight aim (F9 > World, default 10 cm), so a flat-shooting gun's line begins far out.
[*][b]Hide on weapon settings:[/b] the HUD, health and stamina step aside while the weapon settings wheel is open.
[*][b]Hover pack and shield backpack[/b] on the backpack bar: the hover pack's fuel; the shield's strength, orange when low, red when broken.
[*][b]Attachments:[/b] the magazine size and the overheat point are remembered per set of attachments, right from the first shot the next time.
[*]The count no longer slides as it falls: it sits in a box as wide as the full magazine (F9 > Look > Count digits).
[/list]

[size=4][b]NEW in 2.1[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]A landing mark[/b] where a drop or throw line lands: a ring and an arrow on the ground, from the map's own heights (its rocks, trees and props, calibrated by where Helldivers walk, remembered per planet). Faint where the ground is a guess; [b]red[/b] where the shot would hit a bug hole, a fabricator, a warp ship or an Illuminate tower.
[*][b]Aiming read from the game:[/b] toggle aim counts, and the sniper crosshair can show always with the rifle out. The Maxigun gets a crosshair in first person; the flamethrowers can.
[*]The F9 menu regrouped (Layout, Visibility, Look, World, Keys); line opacity and mark size sliders; the Variable's TOTAL icon.
[/list]

[size=4][b]NEW in 2.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Four new layouts[/b] (the horizontal stack, the arcs, the vertical pair, the block) that hold together: parts pin to each other, so hiding or resizing one keeps the rest in order. A Spread slider opens or closes the pair around the crosshair. The editor has pins, snapping, a Share link and a Help page.
[*][b]A rule for every part:[/b] always, while not full, on a change, while aiming, or a mix.
[*][b]Motion:[/b] parts slide, bars ease, short flashes mark the things that matter. Off, quick or normal.
[*][b]Colours now look as they are meant to[/b], on every planet.
[*][b]Emplacement guns[/b] show their rounds while you're seated.
[*]A throw line for [b]every grenade[/b]; bar edges, brackets and shade; the sniper rifles' crosshair in third person.
[*]The settings menu regrouped. Your settings carry over, except the layout: pick yours in F9 (Custom users: Custom layout again; your file is untouched).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Make your own layout[/b][/size]
[b][url=https://luka189.github.io/NarrowHUD/editor/]>> Open the NarrowHUD layout editor <<[/url][/b]
Drag every part anywhere, pin parts to each other, make bars vertical or curved, and see how each weapon will look. Then:
[list=1]
[*]Download your layout file from the editor.
[*]In the game, F9 > NarrowHUD > [b]Layout: Custom layout[/b].
[*]Put the file in [b]%APPDATA%\Arrowhead\Helldivers2[/b] as [b]chef_data_luka189_2fnarrowhud__layout.lua[/b].
[*]Press the "Keys: reload layout file" key, or start a mission.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What it shows[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Ammo[/b]: a tick per round (a bar for big magazines), the count, your spare magazines. Orange when low, red when empty.
[*][b]Heat[/b] and [b]charge[/b] for laser, plasma and charge weapons; [b]lock-on[/b] for the Spear and WASP.
[*][b]Backpack weapons[/b]: what's loaded and what's in the pack.
[*][b]Fire modes, weapon modes, underbarrels[/b] and the rate of fire, as words and as the game's own icons.
[*][b]Stims, grenades[/b], your [b]backpack[/b], [b]health and stamina[/b], a stim timer, reload progress.
[*][b]Emplacement guns[/b] while you're seated.
[*][b]Drop lines[/b] for launchers, rockets and plasma; [b]throw lines[/b] for stratagem balls, grenades and C4; a [b]landing mark[/b] where they land, red on an enemy structure.
[*]Your [b]map pin[/b] and [b]where you last died[/b], with their distance.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Layouts[/b][/size]
The horizontal stack, the arcs, the vertical pair, the block, or your own from the editor. Every part has its own rule for when it shows.

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size] (F9 > NarrowHUD)
Layout, size, offsets, Spread, opacity and motion; Visibility (a rule per part, the aim opacity, stim timer, warnings, reload line, idle dimming); Look (bar edges, shade, colours); World (crosshairs, markers, lines, the landing mark and structure hits); your keys as bound in the game.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Chef Shared Loader[/b] [b]1.2.3 or newer[/b], with the highest priority in your mod manager.
[*]Add the NarrowHUD zip in your mod manager and enable it.
[*]Play. Settings are in F9.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
NarrowHUD [b]only reads[/b] game state through Chef Shared Loader; it never writes to the game. It learns which weapon is in your hand the first time you fire it (most are known already). After a game patch it checks its data and stays off, telling you, if something it can't check has changed.

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]A weapon doesn't show.[/b] Fire it once.
[b]It shows the wrong weapon.[/b] Fire the one you're holding. If it keeps happening: F9 > "Testing: forget this weapon", press it holding the weapon, fire.
[b]I changed my fire button or swap keys in the game.[/b] Set the same under Keys in F9. If your mouse wheel doesn't swap weapons in the game, set "Keys: wheel swaps weapons" to No, or a scroll can switch the HUD to the wrong weapon.
[b]"NarrowHUD is off: the game was updated".[/b] Wait for an update, or turn on "Testing: any game version" at your own risk.
[b]Over the map?[/b] The HUD draws in the game's UI layer so the colours are right; the map is nowhere near it.
[b]Multiplayer?[/b] Yes, hosting and joining. Only your own weapons and pack.
[b]Performance?[/b] About 0.2 ms a frame.

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]PlayStation pads are untested (Xbox-style pads work). 4K, render scale under 100 % and HDR are untested.
[*]The Arc Thrower and the Blitzer have no ammo to show; the Solo Silo's remote shows nothing.
[/list]
Bug reports: see the pinned post (both logs, please).

Changelog for the Nexus "Changelogs" tab (2.4.0)
------------------------------------------------
- Shell flash (F9 > Layout, on by default): each round fired flicks out of the ammo bar in the bar's colour and is
  gone; Shell flash length sets how far it gets.
- Hit trail (F9 > Visibility): what each hit took stays red a moment, then drains away, hit by hit (or as one piece, or
  off); on a red health bar only your health shows. Hit trail held sets how long (0.4 s).
- Warning pulses: a third choice, Flash once (one flash as health turns red, then simply red).
- Shade behind bars: a third choice, Hatched (the game's diagonal stripes behind a bar's empty part, straight and
  curved).
- The reload line runs until the gun is ready (from empty it waits for the chambered round; the AMW-20 Apex and the
  AMR among the weapons it now works for).
- The SG-451 Cookout shows both tubes and the chambered shell, on a bar of 16.
- Firing or holding the trigger with Chef's F9 menu open no longer hides your weapon for the mission.
- The landing mark knows more of the map's ground kinds.

Changelog for the Nexus "Changelogs" tab (2.3.0)
------------------------------------------------
- The weapon in your hand is found by its model at your hand: shown the moment you draw it, before any shot, and two
  weapons on one hand pose (the Nova and the Breacher, a pistol and a rifle on a controller) are told apart without
  a key press.
- The drop line's landing mark uses the game's own aim point: the ground wherever you have looked is known, the
  line aims where the crosshair really points, and a guess for ground you haven't looked at follows the slope.

- Layouts: a part's Side can be Up or Down, so the Spread and the Aim spread move it vertically — the horizontal

- The HUD hides at once (80 ms) for the stratagem key, an emote, the game's menu, the weapon wheel and a terminal,
  and health and stamina go with it in step.
- Your own Helldiver is found by position, not by the camera: an ally passing behind you no longer takes over your
  hand pose or the drop line for a moment.
- The Medium and Thick lines keep their width whichever way they run.
- The magazine size remembered for a set of attachments never shrinks (a weapon with a chambered round starts a
  mission with one fewer in the magazine).
- Switching NarrowHUD off and on in Chef's F9 menu takes effect at once (Chef Shared Loader 1.5).
