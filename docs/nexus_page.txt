NarrowHUD: Nexus Mods page text
================================

Name: NarrowHUD
Version: 1.5.13
Category: User Interface (or Gameplay > HUD, whichever the site lists)
Main file: NarrowHUD-v1.5.13.zip
Requirements (the "Requirements" box): Chef Shared Loader (nexusmods page of Th3chef's loader), "1.2.3 or newer"

Summary (the short line under the title, at most 350 characters):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
A narrow ammo and heat readout by your crosshair: the magazine as a bar, the count, spare magazines, stims, grenades,
backpack, health and stamina. Drop lines for launchers, throw lines for stratagems and grenades, your map pin and where
you died, on screen. Four layouts or your own in the online editor. Reads only. Needs Chef Shared Loader 1.2.3+.

Description (paste into the BBCode editor):
-------------------------------------------
[size=5][b]NarrowHUD[/b][/size]
[i]Narrow enough to not limit the Design Space.[/i]

A small ammo and heat readout right by your crosshair, so you never have to look at the corner of the screen
mid-fight.

[size=4][b]NEW in 1.5: lines and markers in the world[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]A [b]drop line[/b] for grenade launchers, rockets, the crossbow and plasma: where the shot goes, drawn from the
weapon along your aim and bending down as the round drops, hidden where it enters the ground. While you aim down
the sights by default; whenever the weapon is in hand if you like. Every weapon with a visible drop has its numbers.
[*]A [b]throw line[/b] for a stratagem ball, a grenade or a C4 charge: where the throw goes while you aim it, from
your hand to where it lands, your own run carried into the throw.
[*][b]Your map pin[/b] and [b]where you last died[/b] on the screen, with their distance; an arrow at the screen's
edge when out of view.
[*]A fourth layout, the [b]curved pair[/b] around the crosshair, and health and stamina bars on by default.
[*][b]The game's own icons[/b] (1.5.24): the fire mode, the weapon's mode (APHET, HEAT, GUIDED ...) and the underbarrel in
hand as the game's HUD draws them, beside the stims, in the HUD's colour or each mode's own. New stim and grenade icons,
sharp at any size. And four new layouts to preview.
[/list]
All of it in F9 > NarrowHUD, each with its off switch.

[size=4][b]Make your own layout[/b][/size]
[b][url=https://luka189.github.io/NarrowHUD/editor/]>> Open the NarrowHUD layout editor <<[/url][/b]
Drag every part of the HUD anywhere on a game screen: the bar, the count, the magazines, the modes, stims, grenades
and the backpack bar. Make bars vertical or curved, resize or fade any part, and see how each kind of weapon will
look. Then:
[list=1]
[*]Download (or copy) your layout file from the editor.
[*]In the game, F9 > NarrowHUD > [b]Position: Custom[/b] (this creates the layout file the first time).
[*]Put your file in [b]%APPDATA%\Arrowhead\Helldivers2[/b] as [b]chef_data_luka189_2fnarrowhud__layout.lua[/b]
(replace the one there).
[*]Press the key set in F9 under "Read the layout file again", or start a mission.
[/list]
Under, Beside and the curved pair are in the editor to start from, and a screenshot of your own game can go behind the parts.

[size=4][b]What it shows[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Ammo[/b]: a tick per round for smaller magazines (a solid bar for big ones), the count, and your spare
magazines. Orange when low, red when empty, dimmed while reloading. A tactical reload's chambered round gets its own
tick.
[*][b]Heat[/b] for laser weapons, the Quasar and the Nova: the bar, the percentage and a 75 % mark; charge weapons
fill up as they charge. The Double-Edge Sickle runs to 200 % with marks at 50, 100 and 180. Cooling follows the
planet.
[*][b]Backpack weapons[/b] (Autocannon, Recoilless, C4 ...): what's loaded plus the spare ammo in the backpack.
Backpack-fed weapons show the backpack's ammo.
[*][b]Fire modes[/b], [b]weapon modes[/b] (GUIDED / UNGUIDED, APHET / FLAK, HEAT / HE ...), [b]underbarrels[/b] and
the [b]rate of fire[/b] setting, as words and as the game's own icons. Optionally each mode in its own colour, so a
switch is easy to see.
[*][b]Lock-on[/b] (Spear, WASP) and [b]charge meters[/b] (Railgun, Epoch).
[*][b]Stims, grenades[/b], your [b]backpack[/b]'s fill, the warp pack's heat and the jump pack's recharge.
[*][b]Health and stamina[/b] bars: always, or only while they aren't full.
[*]A [b]stim timer[/b], [b]reload progress[/b], low-health [b]warnings[/b], and the HUD out of the way while you call in a stratagem or emote.
[*][b]Your map pin[/b] and [b]where you last died[/b] on the screen, where they are in the world, with their distance; an arrow at the screen's edge when one is out of view.
[*]A [b]drop line[/b] for grenade launchers, rockets and plasma, and a [b]throw line[/b] for stratagem balls, grenades and C4: where the shot or throw goes, as a line in the world.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Four built-in layouts (or your own)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Under the crosshair[/b]: a wide narrow bar, the count and magazines to its right, stims and grenades below.
[*][b]Beside the crosshair[/b] (compact): a short vertical bar, the count, your magazines as small squares (or a
number), the modes, stims and grenades.
[*][b]A screen corner[/b]: by the game's own weapon panel; turn off the bar and items for just the count, magazines and modes (or anywhere
you put it).
[*][b]Curved pair[/b]: the ammo bar as a ) right of the crosshair, the backpack as a ( on the left, the count and
items above, health and stamina over the crosshair.
[*][b]Custom[/b]: your own, from the [url=https://luka189.github.io/NarrowHUD/editor/]layout editor[/url].
[*][b]Preview 1 to 4[/b] (1.5.24): a horizontal stack under the crosshair, a compact block beside it, a curved pair with
every arc centred on the crosshair, and a vertical pair. Previews: they'll replace the layouts above once F9's offsets
move them the way a preset should; the editor has them as presets to start from.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size] (F9 menu > NarrowHUD)
[list]
[*]Size, position (under or beside the crosshair, a screen corner by the game's weapon panel, the curved pair, or Custom from the layout editor), and one offset that moves the built-in layouts anywhere
[*]Every part shown or hidden on its own: bar, count, magazines, HEAT label, modes, fire mode, the game's mode icons, rate of fire, charge meter, stims, grenades, backpack bar
[*]Magazines as squares or a number; the empty squares shown or hidden
[*]Empty parts of the ammo bar, magazines and backpack bar faint, or as outlines
[*]Opacity, and dimming or hiding when nothing has changed for a few seconds
[*]Colours: standard, or colourblind (white, sky blue, yellow, magenta)
[*]The world: the map pin and death marker, the drop line (while aiming, whenever in hand, or off; its length), the throw line, and the lines' thickness
[*]Your fire button, swap keys, the mouse wheel and the weapon options key, if you've changed them in the game
[*]Keys to forget what NarrowHUD learned (about the weapon in hand, or everything)
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Chef Shared Loader[/b] [b]1.2.3 or newer[/b], with the highest priority in your mod manager (older versions block the reads NarrowHUD needs or take some smaller missions for the ship, where NarrowHUD stays off).
[*]Add the NarrowHUD zip in your mod manager and enable it.
[*]Play. Settings are in F9.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
NarrowHUD [b]only reads[/b] game state, through Chef Shared Loader's crash-guarded calls; it never writes anything
to the game.
The game doesn't tell mods which weapon is in your hand, so NarrowHUD watches how your Helldiver holds it and learns
which weapon that is the first time you fire. It ships knowing 95 weapons from testing, so most show straight away;
anything new shows from its first shot. Weapons held the same way (one-handed with a shield, for example) are told
apart by your swap keys.
After a game patch it checks its data against the game and leaves out anything that changed. If it can't check, it
stays off and tells you, rather than risk a crash.

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]A weapon doesn't show.[/b] Fire it once: NarrowHUD learns it from the first shot.
[b]It shows the wrong weapon's ammo.[/b] Fire the one you're holding and it corrects itself. If it keeps happening,
set a key for "Forget what's learned about the weapon in hand" in F9, press it while holding the weapon, and fire.
[b]I changed my fire button or swap keys in the game.[/b] Set the same in F9 > NarrowHUD.
[b]"NarrowHUD is off: the game was updated".[/b] A patch changed something NarrowHUD can't check safely. Wait for an
update, or turn on "Run on a game version it wasn't made for" at your own risk.
[b]Can I use a pixel font?[/b] That's Chef's own setting, for every mod: F9 > Chef Shared Loader > Game font.
[b]Where does it keep what it learned?[/b] chef_data_luka189_2fnarrowhud__learned.lua in
%APPDATA%\Arrowhead\Helldivers2. Its log is narrowhud.log in the same folder.
[b]Does it work in multiplayer?[/b] Yes, hosting and joining. It only shows your own weapons and backpack.
[b]My Custom layout sits somewhere else than in the editor.[/b] Check the two Offset sliders in F9: they move a Custom
layout only when the editor's "F9's offsets move this layout" box was ticked. Also pick your screen's shape in the
editor (16:9, 21:9 ...).
[b]Performance?[/b] About 0.2 ms a frame; up to about 0.5 ms as host in a big mission with SEAF.

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Controllers[/b] (right trigger to fire, Y to swap) work on an Xbox-style pad; PlayStation pads are untested. Reports welcome.
[*]The Arc Thrower and the Blitzer have no ammo to show (only a charge line). The Solo Silo's remote shows nothing.
[*]4K, render scale under 100 % and HDR are untested; 3440x1440, 1080p and non-widescreen are fine.
[/list]
Bug reports: turn on "Research log" in F9 (from the next start), play until it happens, and post narrowhud.log.
