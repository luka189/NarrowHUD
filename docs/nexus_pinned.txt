[size=4][b]Bug reports: please read first[/b][/size]

Thanks for all the reports! To get your problem fixed quickly, please include [b]both logs[/b] from a session where it happened. Without them I can usually only guess.

[size=3][b]1. First, the quick checks[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Chef Shared Loader version:[/b] NarrowHUD needs [b]1.2.3 and newer[/b].
[*][b]Chef Shared Loader must have the highest priority[/b] in your mod manager (then Purge and Deploy). Some mods bring their own boot script (HD2 Transmog, for one): with a higher priority than Chef, theirs replaces Chef's and no Chef mod runs, NarrowHUD included. Chef runs Transmog itself, so both work with Chef on top.
[*][b]No other loader needed:[/b] the "look-alike: CowboyBingusModLoader" line in chef_loader.log is Chef offering other loaders' features to their mods. NarrowHUD only needs Chef.
[*][b]Only in missions:[/b] nothing shows on the ship or in menus.
[*][b]Hidden while you hold the stratagem key[/b] (set yours in F9 > Keys) and while emoting, on purpose.
[*][b]A weapon doesn't show?[/b] Fire it once: NarrowHUD learns it from the first shot.
[*][b]Not showing in the middle of an operation?[/b] Try the next mission, or restart the game. (Being looked into.)
[*][b]Settings missing or moved?[/b] 2.0 regrouped the F9 menu: Layout / Size / Sideways / Up-down / Spread / Opacity, then Show (a rule per part), Look, Extras, Motion, World, Keys, Testing. Your values carried over, except the layout: the old Under / Beside / Corner / Curved pair are replaced by four new layouts, so pick yours in F9 (Custom: pick Custom layout again; your file is untouched).
[*][b]Colours or opacity look different in 2.0?[/b] The HUD is drawn in the game's UI layer now, so the colours are the ones meant and opacity works across its range. "Look: in the game's UI layer" off brings 1.5's in-world look back.
[/list]
